# 01 – Introduction to Deep Learning

## Objective

The objective of this lab was to understand the fundamental concepts of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL), along with their real-world applications and the reasons behind the rapid growth of Deep Learning.

---

## Topics Covered

* Artificial Intelligence (AI)
* Machine Learning (ML)
* Deep Learning (DL)
* Relationship between AI, ML, and DL
* Real-world applications of Deep Learning
* Factors contributing to the success of Deep Learning

---

## Key Learnings

* Artificial Intelligence aims to build systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence.
* Machine Learning is a subset of AI where models learn patterns from data instead of relying on explicitly programmed rules.
* Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses deep neural networks to automatically learn complex representations from data.
* The recent success of Deep Learning is driven by three major factors:

  * Availability of large-scale datasets
  * Powerful computing hardware (especially GPUs)
  * Advances in neural network architectures and training algorithms

---

## Real-World Applications Explored

* **YouTube** – Video recommendation, content understanding, and ranking.
* **Zomato** – Delivery-time estimation and computer vision for operational monitoring.
* **Tesla** – Autonomous driving, lane detection, traffic sign recognition, and object detection.
* **Robotics** – Computer vision, object recognition, and speech understanding.
* **WhatsApp** – AI-assisted features such as spam detection, message suggestions, and safety-related systems.

## Assignment Completed

* Studied the difference between AI, ML, and DL.
* Researched five real-world companies/products using Deep Learning.
* Identified the business problem each application solves.
* Explored the types of deep learning models commonly used in those applications.

---

## Key Takeaways

* Deep Learning is not just another algorithm; it is a learning paradigm capable of solving complex problems by learning hierarchical representations from data.
* Real-world AI systems often combine multiple deep learning models instead of relying on a single architecture.
* Understanding **the problem being solved** is more important than memorizing model names.
